Synthetic PVC Tag

  • Material: Made from synthetic PVC plastic, known for its durability, chemical resistance, and tear resistance.
  • Waterproof & Weatherproof: Resistant to moisture, UV rays, and extreme temperatures, making it suitable for harsh environments.
  • Tear-Resistant: Unlike paper tags, synthetic PVC tags do not rip easily, even under mechanical stress.
  • Printable Surface: Compatible with thermal transfer, laser, and inkjet printing, ensuring high-quality barcodes, text, or graphics.
  • Flexible Yet Strong: Can bend without breaking, yet holds up well under physical stress or wear.
  • Customizable: Available in various sizes, shapes, colors, and thicknesses, often with options for pre-punched holes or adhesive backing.
