Technology We Use

Advanced Machinery for Precision Label Manufacturing



Investing in Innovation for Quality and Efficiency

At Krati Labels and Technology, we invest in cutting-edge machinery to ensure top-tier quality, consistency, and efficiency in every label we produce. Our production line is equipped with high-performance systems that enable us to serve a wide range of labeling needs — from retail and logistics to healthcare and industrial applications.

  • Thermal Roll Slitting & Rewinding Machine

    Used for precise slitting and rewinding of thermal paper rolls, ideal for POS and billing systems.

  • Automatic Die-Cutting & Punching Machine

    Delivers clean and accurate die cuts for custom-shaped labels and tags, including hole punching as per design requirements.

  • High-Speed Flexographic Printing Press

    Capable of multi-color printing on various substrates, offering excellent registration and vibrant label finishes.

  • Thermal Paper Coating & Converting Line

    Ensures smooth coating and conversion of thermal base paper into ready-to-print, high-quality thermal sheets and rolls.

  • Roll-to-Roll Slitter & Sheeter

    Designed for high-precision cutting and trimming of label rolls to specific sizes with clean edges and tight roll alignment.

These machines form the backbone of our production process, allowing us to deliver both standard and highly customized labeling solutions with speed and accuracy.
