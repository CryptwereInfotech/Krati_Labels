Labelling Consumables

  • Compatible with All Major Printers – Suitable for Zebra, TSC, Honeywell, Sato, and more.
  • Multiple Ribbon Types:
    • Wax – For general-purpose printing on paper labels.
    • Wax-Resin – For enhanced durability on coated paper and synthetics.
    • Resin – For high-performance, chemical- and heat-resistant printing on synthetics.
  • Crisp, Long-Lasting Prints – Sharp text, barcodes, and graphics with excellent smudge resistance.
  • Various Widths & Lengths – Custom sizes to fit desktop or industrial printers.
  • Anti-Static & Back-Coated Films – Protects printheads and extends printer life.
  • Ideal for Any Industry – Used in manufacturing, logistics, retail, healthcare, and more.

Holographic & Void Labels

  • High-Security Labeling – Deters tampering, counterfeiting, and unauthorized access.
  • VOID Labels – Leave a “VOID” mark when removed, indicating tampering.
  • Holographic Labels – Eye-catching and difficult to replicate; ideal for brand protection.
  • Customizable Designs – Add logos, serial numbers, barcodes, or QR codes.
  • Durable & Adhesive Options – Strong bond with paper, plastic, metal, and glass surfaces.
  • Ideal for Sensitive & High-Value Items – Commonly used in electronics, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, and official documents.
  • Regulatory & Warranty Use – Supports compliance labeling and product authentication.

Pre-Colored & Color-Coded Labels

  • Easy Visual Identification – Speeds up sorting, tracking, and categorization.
  • Available in a Range of Colors – Standard and custom colors for various workflows.
  • Ideal for Inventory & Workflow Management – Perfect for warehouses, logistics, and healthcare.
  • Supports Custom Printing – Compatible with thermal transfer and direct thermal printing.
  • Multiple Shapes & Sizes – Round, rectangular, and custom die-cut options.
  • Adhesive Flexibility – Permanent, removable, or specialty adhesives to suit all surfaces.
  • Enhances Efficiency & Accuracy – Reduces errors in high-volume operations.

In Mould Label

  • Durable and long-lasting – the label becomes part of the container.
  • Scratch and moisture-resistant – ideal for high-quality packaging.
  • Cost-effective for high volumes – no need for post-labelling.
  • High-quality finish – allows for vibrant, photo-quality graphics.
  • Eco-friendly – label and container are made of the same material, aiding recyclability.

Sleeve Shrink Label

  • 360° Coverage – Allows full-body decoration, including complex container shapes.
  • High Visual Impact – Ideal for vibrant, high-resolution graphics.
  • Tamper Evident – Can include a perforated seal for security.
  • Versatile – Suitable for plastic, glass, and metal containers of various shapes.
  • No Adhesives Required – Labels stay in place using shrink/stretch properties.

Pos Rolls

  • Smooth and high-quality printing surface.
  • Available in multiple sizes and core diameters.
  • Consistent performance in thermal and impact printers.
  • Optional pre-printing with logos, terms, or branding.
  • Eco-friendly and BPA-free options available.

Liquor Label

  • Customizable Designs – Available in metallic finishes, textured papers, embossing, foiling, and clear labels.
